# Methods against the truth

20 synthetic hospitals, 1000 simulated years each for the models and the truth. Each figure is the mean across hospitals ± two standard errors. Truth: the following year simulated from the process that generated the data. 'Days covered': the share of the true year's days on which occupancy is within the number of beds the model recommends.
